Basic Controls

Space: generate a scramble and start inspection. Esc: abort the current solve. Keyboard controls follow a csTimer-style virtual cube layout.

Use the keyboard or the cube's existing touch controls to turn the virtual cube. Key assignments can be changed in Settings → Keyboard Controls.

Solve Flow

  1. Scramble. In Normal virtual-cube mode, press Space to scramble.
  2. Inspect. A 15-second inspection begins. Your first valid move starts the timer. x, y and z whole-cube rotations do not start it.
  3. Solve. The timer stops automatically when the cube is solved. Press Esc to abort if needed.

These timed instructions describe Normal mode. Guided Solve is untimed practice with arrows and step-by-step help; its moves do not count toward normal records or rankings. Battle modes use their own round rules.

ONE SCRAMBLE. YOUR NEXT RIVAL.

What Is Online Cube Battle?

Battle Mode

An online Rubik's Cube battle is a race on a shared scramble. Instead of solving alone against the clock, you turn a virtual cube while another player tackles the same starting position. STcube shows your opponent's cube as the race unfolds, so you can follow the action in real time.

There is nothing to install and no entry fee. Open STcube in your browser, choose a battle type and use the existing cube controls to solve. An internet connection is needed for online play.

How to Start a Battle

  1. Open STcube. Visit the game. Use Google Login for ranked matchmaking, or Guest Login to try a Friend Battle.
  2. Select Battle. Find it in the game's main navigation.
  3. Choose your race. Select Random Battle to find an opponent, or Friend Battle to create or join a room.
  4. Get ready and solve. Follow the room's Ready prompt and inspection countdown, then make your first turn to start your virtual-cube solve.

New to the controls? See Keyboard Controls above. You can change key assignments in Settings → Keyboard Controls.

FIND YOUR NEXT OPPONENT

Random Battle

Choose Find Ranked Match to search for another player. Random Battle requires Google Login and at least one valid completed solve. Matchmaking starts around your rating and broadens its search while you wait.

Humans first. BOT if needed.

Human opponents have priority. If no match is found after 15 seconds, STcube begins switching to a local BOT battle. Preparation can take a little longer. A human match confirmed before the switch wins priority. You can cancel the search.

BOT opponents are clearly labelled BOT with a difficulty level. Their cubes really rotate through a scripted solution. BOT battles are unrated practice: they do not change your rating or count toward human battle statistics.

How ratings and results work

Google players start at 1200. Human ranked results use an Elo-style rating adjustment based on the result and both players' ratings. Repeated matchups can have reduced or no rating impact. The Battle Rating Ranking separates players who have played ranked matches from new players.

When one player finishes, the other has a three-second finishing window. If both reach the two-minute solve limit without finishing, the result uses each player's highest recorded completion score, out of 54 stickers. Equal completion scores produce a draw.

YOUR FRIENDS. YOUR ROOM.

Friend Battle

Want to race someone you know? Log in, open Battle → Friend Battle, then choose Create Room. Share the Room ID with your friends. They can enter that ID under Join a Room and select Join Room.

The room supplies a common scramble, so participants race from the same starting position. For a virtual-cube round, press Ready; the round starts automatically when the participants are ready, followed by inspection. Make your first turn to begin solving.

Friend rooms can include multiple players. Use the room's player list to choose whose cube to watch. Friend Battle does not change your ranked rating, and BOT fallback is not used in friend rooms.

You can also explore the room's real-cube settings if you prefer a physical cube. This guide focuses on racing with the 3D virtual cube.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is STcube free?

Yes. You can use STcube and its battle modes free in your browser. Human ranked matchmaking requires Google Login and a valid completed solve.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a Friend Battle room, share its Room ID and have your friends join. Guest Login is available for Friend Battle. These rooms do not change ranked ratings.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Open STcube in a modern browser. You do not need to install an app or download a separate game to start playing.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. STcube has a responsive interface and touch controls. A desktop keyboard is another option, with customizable key assignments.

How does matchmaking work?

Random Battle searches for human players using rating-based matching. If nobody is found after 15 seconds, it can switch to a clearly labelled, unrated BOT. Friend Battle uses Room IDs instead of automatic matchmaking.
